Our Journey

2018

The First Frame

Yogesh Prajapati captures the first story with a single lens and a grand vision. Lunaro Films was born out of a desire to see emotion through a cinematic eye.

2020

The Expansion

Ajitsinh Rathod joins the vision, bringing operational precision. We moved from local captures to state-wide destination weddings, refining our signature ivory-gold look.

2022

Corporate Narrative

Launched our Corporate & Brand division. We began helping businesses tell their 'Human' stories, merging professional quality with emotional storytelling.

2025

Future Vision

Today, Lunaro Films stands as a premium name in Gujarat. With 500+ stories told, we continue to push the boundaries of cinematic art and high-end visual production.
