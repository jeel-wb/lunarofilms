About Us

Lunaro Films is a creative studio founded by Yogesh Prajapati, specializing in cinematic weddings, pre-wedding stories, and brand films.

With a strong focus on storytelling, emotion, and visual quality, we create films that feel timeless and real.

Supported by Ajitsinh Rathod in operations and execution, we bring passion, precision, and purpose to every frame.
